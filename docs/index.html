OPEN-SOURCE SUPPORT ENGINEERING TOOL

Move support cases from incomplete report to useful engineering evidence.

Support Operations Workbench is a small FastAPI service for safe intake, clear ownership, guarded case states, audit history, and reusable engineering handoffs. It deliberately does not generate or send customer replies.

View sourceRun locally

  • Redacts secrets before storage
  • Calculates priority and SLA deadlines
  • Audits ownership and state changes
  • Exports safe engineering handoffs
WHAT IT DOES

A focused workflow between support and engineering.

The workbench provides enough structure to investigate responsibly without pretending to be a full ticketing platform or autonomous support agent.

Safe intake

Subjects, descriptions, request IDs, and notes are redacted before SQLite receives them.

Operational ownership

Every case has a priority, deadline, status, and optional assignee.

Guarded workflow

Invalid state jumps return 409 Conflict.

Engineering handoff

A Markdown export contains sanitized evidence, ownership, deadline, and audit history.

Runbook guidance

Access, billing, technical, and security cases receive bounded steps.

Product signals

Repeated fingerprints surface patterns without claiming ticket volume proves root cause.

WHO IT IS FOR

Useful when a case needs investigation, not just a response.

Product support engineers

Collect reproducible evidence and prepare clean escalations.

Technical support leads

Track urgency, ownership, overdue work, and patterns.

Developer relations teams

Turn integration problems into documentation or SDK proposals.

Engineering on-call

Receive request IDs, timing, impact, and investigation history.

When it is useful

  • A customer pastes a token into a ticket but useful evidence must be retained.
  • A production failure is moving between teams without an owner.
  • An escalation has a transcript but no request ID, impact, or reproduction.
  • The same symptom keeps returning and needs product investigation.
STEP BY STEP

How a case moves through the workbench

  1. 1

    Capture the report

    Submit the issue, area, impact, environment, and request ID.

  2. 2

    Sanitize before storage

    Credential-shaped values are replaced before any write.

  3. 3

    Calculate urgency

    Visible policy assigns P1, P2, or P3 and an SLA deadline.

  4. 4

    Assign and investigate

    An owner follows the runbook and allowed states.

  5. 5

    Export the handoff

    Engineering receives a safe brief, not a transcript.

  6. 6

    Review recurring signals

    Matching fingerprints identify reports worth validating together.

SAFETY BOUNDARIES

What is guaranteed—and what is not

Implemented

  • Redaction before persistence
  • Audited assignments and transitions
  • Rejected workflow jumps
  • Sanitized exports
  • No customer messaging

Not claimed

  • Complete PII detection
  • Multi-tenant storage
  • User authentication
  • Root-cause proof from fingerprints
  • A ticketing-platform replacement
BEFORE PRODUCTION

What a real deployment still requires

  • Identity: authentication, roles, and tenant isolation.
  • Governance: reviewed PII policy, encryption, retention, deletion, and backups.
  • Reliability: idempotency, concurrency controls, migrations, pagination, and rate limits.
  • Integrations: durable delivery to approved ticketing and incident systems.
  • Operations: logs, metrics, traces, alerts, and recovery procedures.
